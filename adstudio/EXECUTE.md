# EXECUTE — Week One, Hour by Hour

One goal this week: **first paying client by Day 3, three Sprints closed by Day 7,
one retainer conversation started.** Everything below serves that. If a task
doesn't touch prospecting, outreach, or delivery, it waits until week two.

**Daily non-negotiables (Days 2–7), no matter what else happens:**
1. 25 new prospects sourced and logged in `prospecting/pipeline.csv`
2. 25 first-touch messages sent (with real sample ads behind them)
3. All due follow-ups sent (Day 2 / Day 5 / Day 9 touches from the sheet)
4. Every reply answered within 1 hour during work hours
5. Scoreboard filled in before you sleep

A day where all five happen is a won day, even with zero sales. Sales lag
activity by 3–5 days in this system — trust the lag.

## Day 3 (Wednesday) — First close expected

Same skeleton as Day 2 (source 25, send 25, batch samples), plus:

- **8:00 sharp:** send all Day-2 follow-ups due (second free variant — the
  strongest touch in the sequence).
- Any "yes, want the 9 more" reply → send Stripe link **in that same
  conversation, same minute.** Deal momentum dies in hours.
- **The moment someone pays: SOP starts immediately.** Delivery within 48h of
  their intake answers. Nothing this week outranks delivering ad #1 flawlessly —
  cut sourcing to 15 that day if you must, never cut sends to zero.

## Day 4 (Thursday) — Volume + first delivery prep

## Day 5 (Friday) — First delivery day

- **Deliver the first paid batch** (Drive folder + walkthrough + retainer seed,
  exactly per SOP — the upsell goes in the delivery message, not later).
- Full non-negotiables on top. Friday sends land fine; founders clear DMs on
  weekends.
- End of day: pipeline review. You should have ~100 prospects logged, ~100
  touched, 10–20 conversations, 1–3 paid. If conversations < 10, your first
  lines aren't personal enough — fix the first line, not the offer.

- Any active client work per SOP clock (the 48h promise doesn't take weekends).
- Afternoon off. Burnout by Day 6 is how week two dies.

**Week-one targets:** 150 prospects sourced · 150 first touches · ≥15 real
conversations · 3 Sprints closed ($1,491) · every delivery inside 48h · 1
retainer conversation started.

**Honest benchmarks so you calibrate, not panic:** cold outreach with a
personalized free sample converts roughly 10–20% to replies and 2–5% to paid.
At 150 sends that's 3–7 closes on a good week, 1–2 on a slow one. One close
pays for the domain, Stripe, hosting, and Higgsfield many times over — the
system compounds in weeks two to four as follow-ups mature and delivered
clients convert to retainers. The only failure mode that matters in week one
is a day where nothing was sent.
