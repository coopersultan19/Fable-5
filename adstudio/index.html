FAQ

Fair questions.

Are these ads compliant?

Every script goes through a supplement-specific compliance checklist before delivery. We write structure/function claims only ("supports muscle recovery," "promotes restful sleep") and never disease claims ("treats insomnia," "cures anxiety"). Scripts are written to be FTC disclosure-ready, and we flag anything that commonly trips Meta or TikTok's supplement ad policies. To be clear: we're a creative studio, not a law firm — final legal review of your claims is on your side. But our checklist exists so your ads don't get your account flagged for the obvious stuff.

How is this so fast?

We built a production system, not a talent roster. Traditional UGC agencies spend two weeks shipping product to creators, waiting on filming schedules, and chasing re-shoots. Our pipeline goes from your product page to finished, edited vertical video without any of that. The time we save on logistics goes into the two things that actually move performance: hooks and compliance-safe scripts.

What do you need from me?

Your product URL and five minutes on the intake form. No kickoff call, no creative brief, no shipping product. If you have brand guidelines or past winning ads, we'll happily use them — but we don't need them to start.

What formats do I get?

Vertical 9:16 video (1080×1920), optimized for Meta Reels/Stories and TikTok. Each ad is 15–35 seconds with the hook in the first two. Files arrive named by hook and angle so performance is easy to read in Ads Manager.

What if I don't like the ads?

The free sample exists so this never happens blind — you see our quality on your product before paying anything. Sprints include one revision pass on scripts before production. Retainers include two full revision rounds per batch.

Who is this for?

DTC supplement brands spending roughly $10k–$200k/month on Meta or TikTok. If you're founder-led and creative volume is your bottleneck, this was built for you. If you're pre-launch with no ad spend, we're probably not the right fit yet.
