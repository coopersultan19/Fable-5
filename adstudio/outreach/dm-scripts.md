# First-Touch Scripts

Every script is built on the same hook: **you already made them an ad.** You're
not asking for a meeting, a call, or their time — you're handing them something.
Make the free sample ad *before* you send the message. The message is true or it
doesn't get sent.

Rules:
- Personalize the first line with something real from their ads or content
  (that's what the `notes` column in pipeline.csv is for).
- Never say "AI" first. If they ask, answer honestly (see objections.md).
- Send between 8–11am their time. Founders check DMs with coffee.
- One CTA per message. Always the same one: want the ad?

---

## Script 1 — IG DM (founder's personal account) · primary weapon

> Hey [name] — been seeing your [product] ads on my feed. The [specific
> hook/detail, e.g. "gym-bag unboxing one"] is solid.
>
> I run a video studio for supplement brands and made an ad for [product] this
> morning — took me about 20 minutes. Want me to send it? It's yours either way,
> no strings.

**Then, after they say yes and you send it:**

> That's one of ten angles I'd test for [product]. I do 10 like this in 48 hours
> for $497, all compliance-checked so Meta doesn't flag them. Want the other nine?

---

## Script 2 — IG DM (brand account, when you can't find the founder)

> Hey — whoever runs ads for [brand]: I made you a free video ad for [product].
> Not a pitch deck, an actual finished ad. Who should I send it to?

Short on purpose. Brand inboxes are triaged by VAs; the only goal is getting
routed to the founder. When you get a name, switch to Script 1 energy.

## Script 3 — Email (founder's direct email)

**Subject:** made you an ad for [product]

> Hey [name],
>
> I saw [brand]'s ads running in Meta's ad library — looks like the
> [specific creative, e.g. "kitchen-counter testimonial"] has been carrying the
> account since [month].
>
> So I made you a new one. Attached/linked: a finished video ad for [product],
> made this morning. Took me 20 minutes. Use it, run it, no strings.
>
> If it's good, I'll make you 10 more in 48 hours — different hooks, all
> compliance-safe (structure/function claims only, so your ad account stays
> boring and alive).
>
> Want the other nine?
>
> [Your name]
> DoseReel — compliant video ads for supplement brands

Attach nothing on first send if you're worried about spam filters — use a link
to the video (Drive, set to anyone-with-link) and keep the email under 120 words.

## The one metric that matters

**Sample ads delivered.** Not messages sent — samples actually watched by a
founder. Every script exists to earn the right to send the sample. If your reply
rate is under 5% after 50 sends on a channel, your first line isn't personal
enough — fix the first line before touching anything else.
