# Follow-Up Sequence (3 touches: Day 2, Day 5, Day 9)

Rules for every follow-up:
- Each touch **adds something new** — a second ad, an insight about their
  account, a relevant example. Never "just bumping this" or "circling back."
- No guilt, no pressure, no "I know you're busy." You're a peer with something
  useful, not a vendor begging for scraps.
- Same channel as the first touch. If they've opened but not replied twice on
  IG, the day-9 touch can jump to email (note it in pipeline.csv).
- Log every send date in pipeline.csv the moment it goes out.

---

## Touch 1 — Day 2 (+2 days after first message)

**The move: give them a second asset, don't repeat the offer.**

IG/DM version:

> Made a second version of that [product] ad with a different hook — this one
> opens on the [specific angle, e.g. "3pm crash"] instead. Sending both so you
> can see what I mean by testing angles: [link]
>
> Ten of these in 48h is $497 if you want the full batch.

Email version — reply in the same thread:

> Quick add-on: cut a second variant with a different hook ([angle]). Two ads
> now, both free, both yours: [link]
>
> The point of ten variants is exactly this — same product, different doors in.
> Offer stands if you want the batch.

*Why this works: two free ads makes ignoring you feel expensive, and it proves
the "volume of angles" pitch without any explaining.*

---

## Touch 2 — Day 5 (+3 days after touch 1)

**The move: show them something about THEIR account, not your service.**

> Was in Meta's ad library again and noticed [brand]'s been running the same
> [n] creatives since [month]. Fatigue usually shows up as rising CPMs before
> CTR drops — if you're seeing that, fresh angles fix it faster than new
> audiences.
>
> Either way, that's the itch my 10-ad batches scratch. The two free ones I sent
> are still yours to run.

*Personalize the observation from your pipeline.csv notes. If you can't say
something true about their account, say something true about their sub-niche:
"Sleep brands are getting hammered on CPMs this quarter — everyone's running
the same 'tired woman at 3am' creative."*

---

## Touch 3 — Day 9 (+4 days after touch 2)

**The move: close the loop cleanly, leave the door open, zero neediness.**

> Last one from me — I'm keeping my client list small so I can hold the 48-hour
> turnaround, and I'm filling the last couple slots this month.
>
> The two [product] ads I made are yours regardless. If creative volume becomes
> the bottleneck later, you know where I am.

Then mark the row `dead` in pipeline.csv. **Do not send a fourth touch.**
Re-approach dead prospects after 60 days with a brand-new sample ad and a fresh
first line — never "following up on my old messages."

---

## If they reply at any point

Kill the sequence immediately and respond like a human within the hour if you
can. Speed-to-reply is the highest-leverage free thing in this whole system.
Move the conversation toward one question: "Want the other nine?" — then take
payment via the Stripe link, no calls unless *they* ask for one.
